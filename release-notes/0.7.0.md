## Highlights

### A first run that gets you to a design

The first-run page is rebuilt: the setup code sits at the top with a copy button, and the advanced options are collapsed until you want them. A new hub shows a short strip that points to the first steps, and empty lists link to the guide instead of sitting blank. The starter bodies and designs now carry part numbers.

### Module slots

Modules (the cable families, connector libraries and signal sets) now declare where they plug in. The Library Import menu lists those places, installing a module shows progress in a drawer, and a restart asks before it happens.

### Wiring on the canvas

- Connect a new design by signal, by colour, or leave it open, and watch a live preview as you choose.
- Wire from the keyboard through the connection table; the canvas respects reduced motion.
- New layouts keep open ends on the outside with the wire between them.
- Connector nodes always list their pins with 16 px targets; the art is a thumbnail and a face toggle, and dense faces enlarge to hit the same size.
- The node creator groups parts by kind and offers matches from your stores.
- A design now warns about conductors left floating at both ends, and names terminals in words.

### One design system, light and dark

Every screen draws from the same set of tokens and components, in light and dark, with contrast checked against AA. Copy across the app was swept so it no longer shows file paths, environment variables or internal names.

### Documents on one frame

Every sheet (build sheet, bill of materials, continuity spec, drawing, formboard, labels, schematic and the wire spec sheet) shares one border, title block and corner state stamp, on the paper you choose in Settings, with an ISO or ANSI title block. The state stamp reads UNRELEASED or UNAPPROVED until a version is released. Text is set in IBM Plex, and PDFs are vector with real text. The Documents preview shows the sheet at paper size, with zoom.

Without a PDF engine the headless PDFs now carry the same frame and the same type, and the build sheet's end pages lay the strip diagram out at a steady size instead of floating on a half-empty page.

### Labels for your printer

Wire labels now read Source end and Destination end, carry the design's part number and revision, and can print a QR code (the part number and revision, or an address you set in Settings › Documents). Choose the label stock in Settings or per print: the A4 and Letter sheets, or one label per page for common Brady and Dymo sizes.

### Documentation inside the app

The guide, the reference and a search now live on the docs site. Help links in the app open the page for what you are looking at, and Ctrl K searches the docs too.

### Words

A design is the noun everywhere (not cable). The two ends of a wire are the source end and the destination end; the part number field is Reference; and the list is Find a design.
